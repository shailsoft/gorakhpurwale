# Phase 5/7 — owner guide

## Everyday administration

- Products: edit title, facts, real images, category, attributes, variations, SKU, stock and price in Products. Remove all `[DEMO]` items before launch.
- Storefront: Appearance → Customize → GW Storefront controls announcement, hero and verified support/social details. Homepage collections use normal WooCommerce products.
- Customization: edit a product → Product data → General → Enable tee designer/back print. Global fees, upload gate, limits and retention live under WooCommerce → Settings → Products → GW Tee Designer.
- Orders: WooCommerce Orders shows validated design metadata and controlled artwork downloads. Move custom orders through Artwork review, Customer action needed, Printing, Packed, Shipped and Completed as operations justify.
- Discounts: use WooCommerce coupons. Never hard-code fake sale badges; sale display follows real regular/sale prices.
- Import/export: use WooCommerce product CSV tools; test imports on staging and preserve SKUs.

## Marketing, SEO and privacy

WordPress/WooCommerce provide clean titles, canonical/product data through compatible SEO tooling, media `srcset`, sitemap support and product exports. Add one vetted SEO plugin only if operational needs justify it. Connect Search Console after the domain/security audit. Add analytics, newsletter, WhatsApp and social links only with real accounts, consent/privacy controls and verified contact details.

## 30-day care

Daily: failed payments/webhooks, order email failures, low stock and artwork review queue. Weekly: disk headroom, cleanup job, updates on staging, backup completion and security/admin review. Monthly: test restore, refund path, accessibility spot-check, Core Web Vitals, 404/search spam, stale temporary assets, retention and account access.
